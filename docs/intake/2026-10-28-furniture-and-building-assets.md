## Library furniture: FC-fb6e20a-library-furniture

**Source:** Farmyard **`fb6e20aa6e7d0cfe8793b4b7f693552d69a3a18b`**, published on `codex/library-furniture` and being reviewed separately from main. Entries are `sites/tactical-animal/dist/art/library/{catalog.js,catalog.json,furniture.js,book-spines.svg}`, with `docs/art/library/README.md` and `sites/tactical-animal/tests/library-furniture.test.mjs`. The evidence lists the declared inherited source/texture dependencies. The atlas uses original painted book spines alongside existing AFT material/cargo paints and primitive recipes.

Forms include tall/wide/low cabinets, double-sided cabinets, plain/ornate full-tile 1 × 2 cabinets, and 1 × 2 / 2 × 4 reading tables. Finishes are oak, walnut, sage, ivory, oxblood and graphite. Quarter-turn reservations, actual navigation footprints, placement offsets and exact solid-part matrices are supplied. These hooks do not place furniture in a mission or integrate the frozen designer.

**Accuracy:** Individual book meshes better represent protruding spines, gaps and close-up silhouettes. Painted shelf rows deliberately replace them with simple solid boxes; painted gaps are not physical holes. Prefer actual body reservations and solid geometry over opaque full-tile blockers, especially below tables. Keep paper, wood and metal as explicit consumer material assignments; a finish name or colour is not a measured combustion property.

**Efficiency:** Prefer painted shelf rows for normal tactical/isometric distances. The donor's geometry counts are 216–528 triangles for the original four stocked cabinet forms versus 3,640–11,984 previously, a reported 93.6–95.6% reduction. New full-tile plain/ornate cases use 600/1,560 triangles and nine material batches each. Shared geometries/maps and per-root instancing reduce repeated geometry work, but do not establish cross-root batching or a frame-time gain. No CPU/GPU benchmark was run. Retain detailed individual books only where close interaction needs them.

**Narrative:** Subjective preference: the inspected plain/ornate comparison retains legible shelves and period character. Use plain cabinets as the common economical form and ornament where the room's status matters. Empty/stocked states support searched rooms without requiring a detailed book simulation. Tinderbox can later model aggregate shelf fuel if its fire design calls for it; visual stock must remain distinct from authoritative inventory/fuel.

**Acceptance and suggested split:** Coder reviews stable IDs, caller/library/instance ownership, body versus solid-part geometry, quarter-turn transforms and explicit unsupported adapters. Editor/UI add a namespaced catalog with finish/content filters, correct offsets and incoming/usable status. Artist performs a bounded scale/appearance check of a table and both full-tile cabinets, without redesigning the pack. Prove under-table rays, both cabinet faces, repeated load/remove/dispose and partial texture-load failure. The **11 source tests passed independently**. The source gallery uses a software renderer because WebGL was unavailable; GPU shading/performance remains an adoption gate.

The factory's roots contain instanced meshes. The accepted halo pipeline explicitly rejects batched targets. Do not silently make these searchable halo targets: either add a separately reviewed per-instance silhouette path or report that cue as unsupported. Reuse Nautilus's Three namespace and current optional-renderer packaging rather than loading a second renderer or expanding the headless core's closure.

## Hopkins houses: TB-b61fa09-hopkins-houses

**Source:** Tinderbox **`b61fa093e3491fb2f109f1f4b0ca5591d2637cc9`**, published on `codex/art-assets`, not master. `assets/art/hopkins-housing-v1/` contains twelve designs 9–20; `hopkins-housing-v2/` adds fifteen designs 21–35. The latter has **29 published levels, 181 labelled spaces and 146 centered window bays** in the donor's review. Together with the thirteen Gross houses the donor has forty designs; those Gross houses are not forty new Nautilus assets.

Review `src/art/{hopkins-housing.js,hopkins-neighborhood.js,gross-housing.js,pattern-roofs.js,gambrel-roof.js,nautilus-windows.js,resources.js}`, both collection manifests/house plans, their source records and focused review files. Six existing textures retain their bytes and metre repeats; this is not a new bitmap-texture set. The window closure is pinned to Nautilus **`90054d07134116a5d9661f8318a76fe68f922aad`**. Reconcile that older shared-kit pin with the current window API rather than copying another engine into itself.

**Accuracy:** Prefer the source-labelled plans and native dimensions over generic building envelopes when period rooms and bays matter. These are pattern-book interpretations, not surveyed reconstructions. Grid adjustments, simplified stairs/closets, roof pitches and omitted elevations remain labelled. The new roof work removes buried faces and roof holes; vertical closures keep wall siding while slopes use roof covering. The Gross material correction postdates Nautilus's `6693bfa` pin; verify whether the engine provider already compensates before backporting it. Do not replace immutable vendor bytes or silently redirect a saved collection identity.

**Efficiency:** Prefer a small, lazy-loaded provider selection with exterior presentation for settlement authoring; retain furnished cutaways as an explicit richer profile. Donor review counts for the latest fifteen houses total 22,888 mesh nodes and 1,215,187 position vertices. Those are geometry counts, not city performance measurements. The revised intersecting roof assembly addresses geometry/correctness; it does not by itself establish faster rendering. Reuse paints and the window kit, benchmark multiple houses at intended camera sizes, and decide LOD/culling/batching from evidence.

**Narrative:** Subjectively, the inspected six-house sheet adds useful porch, bay, gable and silhouette variety for Tinderbox's streets. Prefer these source-specific houses for that period; preserve the existing broader original catalog for other settings. Descriptive interiors do not establish traversable rooms, working stairs, openings, glass breakage or burning buildings.

**Acceptance and suggested split:** Coder/Editor define a new immutable provider identity and a bounded import closure. Start with a bay house, a high-attic cottage and an open-pier foundation; preserve collection IDs, bounds, texture metres, window apertures, floor pitch, cutaways, disposal and explicit consumer rejection. Artist checks roof intersections, vertical siding, source/adaptation labels and opening anchors. UI extends existing collection filters/picking and displays the actual integration limits. Check round-trip references and package-relative resources. The v2 checker writes donor review JSON, so the scout **did not rerun it in the read-only donor**. Its source-owner results are attributed; committed manifest hashes were independently verified. No new live GPU acceptance is claimed.

## New York State Building: TB-b689f55-new-york-building

**Source:** Tinderbox **`b689f5501ab06f54822f2aa724ac77de106e3b71`**, published on `codex/new-york-state-building-art`. Keep `assets/art/exposition-new-york/` and `exposition-new-york-tile/` as separate editions. The original is preserved at **`5e8d64b8b41637637d57ed762eb5157e5d521b4f`**. Read both `docs/art/.../handoff.md` records, tile `plan.js`, manifests, metadata and the source measurement/adaptation register. Tile runtime dependencies include the original directory's unchanged `forms.js` and `native-contract.js`; copying only the new folder is incomplete.

**Accuracy:** Favor the original study for source-proportion comparison. The recorded 154 ft frontage is 46.9392 m: the original uses 47 m, the grid edition 48 m. Derived from donor measurements, those differences are approximately 0.13% and 2.26%; these are not fresh survey measurements. The grid copy deliberately changes depth/rear dimensions and level heights. Favor its explicit native wall/floor recipe for future game-grid adoption. Both preserve custom curves/ornament and acknowledged historical uncertainties. Neither proves structural/fire accuracy or historical colours.

**Efficiency:** The original donor records 177,872 triangles/166 instance batches; the grid check produces 249,708 triangles/207 batches, including 6,744 floor tiles. Grid conformity is therefore **not a demonstrated rendering optimization**; its triangle count is about 40.4% higher. Choose the original for a lighter visual study when simulation floors are unnecessary. Before adopting the grid copy, review a stripped exterior/LOD profile and measure a representative scene. Instanced floors share geometry but still have simulation/visibility costs if later enabled.

**Narrative:** The inspected model and comparison sheet preserve a distinctive civic landmark in both profiles. Prefer the source study for archival display and the grid edition for a game requiring consistent occupied levels, subject to the capability gate below. Do not collapse them into one saved identity or globally resize the original.

**Acceptance and suggested split:** This is an architecture decision before a normal provider import. Its highest slab is **L15 / 31.80 m**, while Nautilus has six editable map layers. A visual whole-building profile could be considered separately, but it must not imply sixteen-level editing, traversal or collapse. The architect must choose an explicit supported landmark profile or a versioned height/floor extension; unsupported uses must reject. Coder checks that boundary, placement/rotations, dependency closure and borrowed resource ownership. Artist reviews the existing measured/adapted profiles rather than remaking the landmark. UI labels the two editions and unsupported capabilities.

The scout reran the tile checker **without `--package`**, preserving donor files and starting no server: 41 original files unchanged, 24 wall runs, 1,732 wall fragments, 6,744 floor tiles, 1,590 closure strips, four poses/appearance checks and 541 disposal checks passed. Physical collision, interior partitions, stair movement, fire/collapse, final placement and actual WebGL acceptance remain open.
