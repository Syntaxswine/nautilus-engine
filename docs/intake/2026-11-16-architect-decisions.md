Define cache invalidation for painting, clearing, undo, reload and map replacement. A new capability must describe actual supported presets/levels and refuse unsupported inputs before live mutation; a label alone grants no support.
- Exact complete save/restore for the supported stance, partial movement, awareness and resources; deliberate legacy/mismatch handling without refilling resources or replaying one-shot effects. Unsupported slopes, arbitrary rigs, upper floors and equipment combinations remain explicit.

Prove an early narrow path on one existing source-supported native rig, flat ground, empty hands and one rifle fit: stand to prone, move, stop, reverse, meet an obstruction, save/reload and resume. UI later owns only the stance/mode indicator and cover feedback against the fixed API. Two-host parity and the bounded Tinderbox foliage extension remain later acceptance gates before claiming general consumer adoption; Tinderbox's current refusal stays until its adapter is proven.

**Design choice:** AFT's exposure, movement and hearing model is preferable for stealth-oriented tactical play because those causes support meaningful cover choices. It is a game abstraction without physical calibration. Farmyard's simple range factor remains cheaper by code inspection and stays available for compatibility. Ground contacts and signed accepted travel are preferable to input-driven animation, without claiming a measured performance benefit. The visible low crawl through authored brush is the narrative target, judged subjectively.
