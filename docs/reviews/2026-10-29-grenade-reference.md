## Design recommendation

**Accuracy:** prefer the source-exact profile for Farmyard outcomes and original rig/anchors for art consistency. This does not calibrate ballistic physics. Keep the measured hand/arc gap and cosmetic bridge explicit; richer worlds need complete reactive profiles.

**Efficiency:** optional public boundaries, shared Three, selected assets and unchanged-pose caching avoid a second game/renderer. Request sizes and allocation counts above are measurements; practical CPU/GPU/device performance remains unmeasured.

**Narrative:** original paint, pin/plant/release/follow-through and green/brass controls produce a coherent interaction (subjective judgment). Prefer native presentation for this proof, with an explicit reduced option. Preserve command-time truth; games own cast, audio, equipment, body services and whole-game persistence.
