## Accepted scope

Accept the private headless level-rectangle composition profile and its one
complete-bundle preparation/retained-consumer path. One explicit composition
group contains at most sixteen independently placed D4 rectangles, with at most
32 total checked owners. Original recipes, controls, IDs, placements, seeds,
anchors, locks and unused source remain authoritative. A saved complete finish
order selects visible finish; all contributing owners and original intervals
remain represented. Cosmetic changes copy only the selected owner's recipe.

The compiler reuses C1 rational arithmetic, arrangement and component/void/sector
topology. It emits labelled occupied partitions, native source/port intervals,
specific primary reservation certificates and complete source/context bindings.
Whole-owner source and visible coverage locks, old-plus-new dependency closure,
one-use handles and final retained-host CAS remain atomic. Unknown acceptance
keeps the original request identity for host-owned durable recovery.

This is source/private-package acceptance. It assigns no public v2 export,
schema registry entry or capability, changes no normal whitelist/version/pin,
and grants no renderer, Editor, consumer or Sites adoption. All eighteen accepted
v1 runtime/schema files remain exact. The separate public-v1 tile renderer is
still the implemented surface provider; it does not render C2a partitions.
General Boolean curves/fields, variable widths, graded or mixed-level geometry,
ramps/stairs/solids, physical queries/support/navigation/fire and arbitrary
transitions remain unresolved or explicitly refused.

## Focused source corrections

The original source derived external contacts from reservations alone. A
reservation-free or displaced external rectangle could therefore cover a locked
member without a reported contact. The witness moves an external [3,5] x [0,2]
rectangle to [1,3] x [0,2] over locked [0,2] squared: exactly 2 square metres of
uncomposed overlap. The correction independently checks actual placed geometry
for every owner pair and includes old/new actual bounds in dependency closure.
Only a specific supported geometric relation can admit contact; one primary
certificate cannot waive another reservation. Refusal preserves next:null,
changes:null, source, history and redo. Nine targeted geometric cases plus two
lowered-work refusals passed the independent correction recheck.

The original internal root name repeatedly appended .root and could exceed the
existing 96-character schema bound. The correction tries c2a/root/0 through
c2a/root/32 with each lookup/allocation precharged. At most 32 owners guarantee
an available name of at most eleven characters. Independent suffix/collision
fixtures and reversed owner orders reproduce deterministic unique names; lowered
work refuses before allocation. The 18/32-owner naming fixtures are static
compiles, not a claim that their old-plus-new preparation bypasses the unchanged
128-original-segment ceiling.

Host/source, mathematical and budget reviews found no remaining must-fix in
these bounded correction classes. A separate finite review of Root's source
fence, schema exceptions and CI/installation orchestration found no integration
blocker. It did not replace the combined execution below.

## Preference and lifecycle

For joined Rectangle-tool paving and footprints in all three games, prefer one
explicit labelled union with retained finish owners over hidden overlapping
tiles or a second editor-side matcher. Accuracy here means exactness relative to
original binary64 source and native contracts, not physical calibration. Existing
isolated curved paths and legacy profiles remain compatible.

Efficiency is bounded rather than benchmarked: one shared arrangement/preparation
budget, a maximum 496 actual owner pairs per checked layout, and fixed arithmetic,
partition/provenance/output ceilings. The extra geometry checks have real cost;
work counters are not CPU instructions or phone frame-rate measurements. The
subjective authoring preference is broad reusable parametric families that retain
independent finishes and reliable locked neighbours. A larger catalogue should
grow through these contracts; every proposed geometry family still needs proof.

All foreground proof/gate commands exited successfully. Both exact orchestrator
PID/creation/executable identities are absent. The private installer removed its
verified task-prefixed stage/consumer in finally. This integration started no
server, browser, watcher or listener and retains no helper. No tag/release,
consumer pin or audience change occurs.
