The kernel retains complete authored field/region/profile-sweep descriptors and
line/arc/cubic/compound controls, frames, metre widths/heights, template pins,
anchors, locks and variant/selection inputs. Derived canonical borders preserve
actual positions, normals, UVs, material intervals, full analytic/evaluator
identity and fixed schedules. Boundary parameter and route-flow tangent policies
are distinct. Multiple spans/layers, diagonal and top/bottom faces, explicit
open/terminal/required ports and complete required-port coverage are exercised.
Direct transitions are explicit recipes, not guessed material/UV blending.

The field model has exact border fade/derivative behavior and explicit guarded
saddle/D4/complement metadata. A world corner registry checks corner-only
height/scalar/paint contacts; field ports use the same whole-shape boundary data.
Geometric edge identity excludes incidental owner IDs/skin/detail. Both matched
ports keep geometry; resident accepted interfaces gate skirt suppression and
the host retains any future support authority.

Pure prepare/repair validates revision/layout/catalogue/context tokens, manual
locks and bounded old/new spatial dependencies across finite alternatives.
Incomplete new-neighbour semantics refuse. Crossings measure actual reservation
clearance, alongside measures the declared lateral gap, transitions need an
accepted interface, and layer/tag names never waive positive-volume conflicts.
Results include old and new affected anchors plus exact changed/removed IDs.
Search, spatial and aggregate layout/candidate geometry work are independently
bounded; caller data and redo-equivalent accepted state survive all refusals.

Self-contained bundles preserve unique drawn controls without presenter caches
or external catalogue availability. Opening always revalidates joins; saveable
authored/unresolved data is distinct from requireResolved live adoption. The
small retained consumer delegates one atomic undo entry to the host and fences
complete host/context/content identity immediately before writing. Injury,
spent resources, queues, clocks, random streams and unknown retained data remain
host-owned. False, unknown and positive acknowledgements are distinguished.

The 128 recipe/instance cap counts semantic authored objects in an atomic chunk,
not metre cells. A single 400 x 4 m region produces 1,600 derived cells; a 64 m
cubic produces 256 cells over separately sampled windows with identical shared
cuts across interior-detail changes. These internal cuts are sampling aids, not
another join/support authority. Canonical external ports remain mandatory mesh
constraints and a provider must stitch to them or refuse.

## Focused correction for Architect recheck

This pass preserves 4a history on the existing branch. Architect explicitly
approved refusal of unsupported ambiguous field conditioning, complete compound
endpoint accounting and per-geometric-contact repair proof. No root registration,
new schema ID/capability, renderer/editor change or consumer adoption is included.
Accepted window head remains 573f18b2017eb7ccc6ce68ec64a20e16871416ea.

- Field model: masks 5/10 require exactly [1,-1,1,-1] or [-1,1,-1,1]. The reported
  [1,-1000,1,-1000] counterexample and asymmetric/extreme/small-positive/zero
  checkerboards refuse unchanged in evaluation, topology, normal fields,
  recipes/bundles and all schema embeddings. General non-checkerboards remain
  supported. Full selected-diagonal path/barrier reasoning bounds the signed
  bubble coefficient below by 2 for every allowed amplitude/seed. Regressions
  exercise both saddle choices, nonzero amplitude extremes, D4/complement and
  both signs. Complement revalidates after negation, preserves derived zero as
  positive zero and refuses [0,1,0,1]'s unsupported negated conditioning.
- Compound curves: every original start is measured, including omitted nodes.
  The reported 0.4 micrometre gap now fails a 1e-12 m tolerance. Total returned
  error includes analytic interpolation plus the larger of nearest-node
  quantization and skipped-start deviation from the actual retained node.
  Independent interpolation checks cover both primitive orders, all D4 choices
  and translated grid phases; sampler/layout/repair work charges the extra starts.
- Repair: exact per-contact geometry/placement/floor/reservation fingerprints
  replace old pair exemptions. Cosmetic interior data, anchors, locks and content
  versions do not invalidate unchanged contacts. Each changed/new contact needs
  its own measured relation or actual finite joined boundary. Cross/alongside
  and transition/join pair waivers are all covered by regressions, including
  additional contacts, changed side/rotation/shape/height/floor, finite segment/
  thickness and channel/layer identity, old/new closure and fixed locks.
  Automatic join proof is limited to continuous straight axis-aligned lateral
  spans and thickness. Valid curved/diagonal/cap or gapped joins remain authored/
  compiled data; an actionable INCOMPLETE_REPAIR requests a specific measured
  relation or supported finite boundary. This limit is prominent in the guide.
