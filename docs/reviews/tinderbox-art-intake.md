`loadTinderboxArt(THREE,{families:['waterfront']})` and `loadTinderboxMedicine(THREE)` execute while interior/plan imports are forbidden in a meaningful module-load check. No million-triangle exterior factory is a dependency. Native props and their physical-metre appearance bindings remain available without constructing any layout. Independent medicine snapshots reject invalid/duplicate IDs before changing presentation, hide consumed/omitted IDs even while paused and never change model scale to match pickup radius.

Prefer the small independent props and waterfront kit for general native modular authoring. Their realistic scale and explicit openings improve geometric accuracy over stretched placeholders. Authored dimensions, inferred room divisions and photographic interpretations remain labeled. Optional construction and per-asset instancing avoid a donor-game dependency; catalogue triangles and resource counts are geometry measurements, not frame-rate benchmarks. Warm period finishes and coherent joinery suit the existing painterly library (subjective art judgment). Full landmark exteriors, legacy studies, medicine/game policies, exterior-halo rendering and fire simulation are deferred; they require distinct source and consumer reviews.

The four arrangements preserve 65 descriptive spaces, 64 partition runs and 386 placed furnishings. They supply no exterior shells, floor slabs or stairs. Matching shell pins and replacement IDs remain explicit in the source handoff. The donor's single-bullet tank instruction remains source evidence; this engine intake supplies only reversible intact/destroyed presentation and does not select a shared damage rule.
