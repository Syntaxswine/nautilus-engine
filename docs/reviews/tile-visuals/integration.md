## Design preference and remaining work

For reusable surface tools, prefer canonical source controls, explicit complete
boundary/material bindings and whole-bundle saves. Accuracy is native/source/seam
agreement, without a physical simulation claim. Efficiency evidence is the small
optional closure, shared existing atlas, bounded work/triangle policy and consistent
ownership; target-device timing remains unmeasured. Painterly materials and smooth
paths support the shared visual direction (design judgment); each game retains its
own simulation and narrative profiles.

Smart Tile Editor chrome, a complete retained-map checkpoint/journal and real
consumer adoption remain separate. Authored curves do not imply general automatic
repair. Boolean composition, variable widths, native sections, graded surfaces,
stairs and building/roof adapters retain their explicit staged status. No new
schema/capability, version/release tag, consumer pin or Sites audience change occurs.

**17 historical local browser/server pairs**, **34 original PID/creation/executable
identities** and **17 released listening ports**, including the first failed source run.
