## Design preference and cleanup

For all three games' authoring, prefer one lossless base-map wrapper and explicit
native overlays over destructive wall replacement or embedded runtime pane saves.
Accuracy here is native/source agreement and preservation, not a physical-glass
claim. Efficiency is bounded by the documented wrapper/history/placement limits;
the static closure above does not establish mobile speed. Narratively (design
judgment), retaining the original wall underlay makes edit/remove/undo predictable
and lets future games choose their own presentation and gameplay bindings.

Temporary installed stages/consumers/comparison fixtures were removed in the
verified `finally` path. All foreground gate/proof children exited. No local
browser/server/watcher/worker/port or retained helper was created for this
registration. No donor/game pin, release tag, deployment or Sites audience change.
