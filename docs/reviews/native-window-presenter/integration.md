## Design preference, limits and cleanup

For Farmyard tactical windows, prefer the source-native frames, pane masks and
painted skins over stretching decorative sash bays. Accuracy is agreement among
source, compiled openings, native meshes and complete host queries; this is not
a calibrated glass, penetration, fire or structural model. Efficiency is bounded
by the accepted placement/pane/wall/view limits and measured geometry/resource
counts; no device FPS or physical-phone timing claim is made. Native framing and
durable quiet holes improve tactical readability (design judgment). Preserve the
period sash library for dressing and game-specific material/fire policies behind
consumer adapters.

This is reusable authoring-preview/runtime presentation, not Editor chrome or
game adoption. Games still supply six real live services, complete world queries,
confirmed impacts, costs, clocks and retained saves. Upper-floor live handoff,
arbitrary provenance/free rotations, framed resizing, shatter and audio remain
outside this tranche. No new schema/capability, consumer pin, version/release tag,
Sites publication or audience change occurs.

All three disposable browser/server pairs closed through supported APIs with a

creation-time and executable identities had ended, all three listening ports were
