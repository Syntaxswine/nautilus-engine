## Design decision

For Farmyard's tactical windows, prefer the accepted source-native dimensions,
joins and inexpensive painted skins over stretching older decorative bays.
Accuracy here means source/compiled/native mesh agreement, not calibrated glass
physics. The measured comparisons and exact clips preserve openings; shatter,
penetration, fire and structural engineering are absent.

Efficiency is bounded by code: ≤1,024 placements, ≤6 panes each, ≤4,096 legacy
walls, one active plus two candidate views. The optional three 128² RGBA wall
maps total 196,608 host-owned bytes. Independent per-placement geometry trades
some geometry reuse for clear retirement ownership. Workload/triangle/resource
counts are evidence; no device FPS, physical accuracy or gameplay-performance
claim is made. Native borderless panes have four triangles and no side seams.

Narratively (artist judgment), native steel/enamel framing and quiet durable
holes make tactical outcomes readable. Preserve the decorative period sash
library for architectural dressing; it serves a different visual/semantic role.
Tinderbox-specific fire/material response remains a separate design decision.
