# Castle / cyclone art provenance

The `nautilus-fort-era-variants-v1` constructions, module geometry, authored detailed/reduced meshes, assemblies, diagrams, exports and review renders were created for Nautilus Engine. They are original fictional game-scale castle and modern security variants of the fixed frontier artist kit at `6d9dfeca7e47a4bded6497472f46f49f8ce14a5b`.

The new material atlas was generated with built-in ImageGen and copied byte-for-byte into the project. `material-prompt.json` records the full prompt, output identity, tile layout and generation mode. The existing original shared painterly atlas supplies additional castle timber surfaces. No reference photographs or photographic pixels were used in the new atlas, wire geometry or review images.

The unchanged frontier recipe, layout and connector sources, and the measured-building compiler closure, retain their source attribution and fixed commit history. New mesh, roof, renderer, exporter and review code derive from the reviewed artist foundation and add their own constructions. Pinned Three and other engine foundations retain their existing notices; see the repository `NOTICE.md` and the original frontier collection's provenance. This notice does not grant rights in earlier third-party reference material.

The collection is descriptive art pending shared editor intake. Native dimensions and connector clearances do not assert historical accuracy, industrial specifications, structural ratings or simulation support.
