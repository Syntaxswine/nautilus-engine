# Original bathroom kit

Original procedural bathroom fixtures, enclosures, partitions, motion rigs and layouts by the Nautilus artist task. Authored dimensions are game-scale choices, not product measurements or regulatory guidance.

The original painterly atlas was generated with built-in ImageGen. The complete prompt history and selected output are recorded in material-prompt.json. No reference photographs or commercial models were used.

The surface primitive dependency is the unchanged packages/fort-variants/geometry.js at artist commit 65c06735396e14b8774ae1e7d5ad61969692963f. The optional host Three runtime remains separately licensed in its original pinned vendor directory; it is not copied into this art bundle.

Shared editor provider/persistence and game adoption remain separate from visual asset creation. No existing fort assets, donor bytes, pins or public release exports are changed.
