# Frontier fort art provenance

The `nautilus-frontier-fort-v1` recipes, fictional plans, module geometry, authored detailed/reduced meshes, generated exports, assembly configurations, connector descriptions and review renders were made for the Nautilus Engine project. They are original game-scale adaptations of a broad frontier-fort vocabulary. No historic accuracy, surveyed dimensions or structural rating is asserted.

The user supplied the Paragon Scenics Fort Apache product page as a visual starting point. Its page, descriptions and photographs remain third-party reference material. The reference register records URLs and inspected hashes; those photographs and page pixels are excluded from the repository, textures, review renders and art bundle. No license to distribute the reference material is asserted.

`painted-post-materials-v1.png` is a byte-identical copy of the original built-in ImageGen output preserved with the earlier real-post prototype. `painted-stockade-wood-v1.png` is a new original built-in ImageGen output. The prompt records preserve their selected output names, original prompts, generation mode and use. The existing original `assets/buildings/painted-materials-v2.png` atlas supplies additional stone, timber, floor and iron tiles. These are generated original materials, not photographic collages.

The collection reuses the unchanged measured-building compiler (`index.js` and `designs.js`) from artist foundation `8631b0cfc6005f3004956afa50ec0e46e5f69278`, and derives its artist mesh/roof/adapter/export implementation from that reviewed collection. The pinned Three runtime, engine baseline and approved temporary review harness retain their existing notices. See the repository `NOTICE.md`; this document does not replace third-party terms or grant rights in the Paragon source material.

This art collection is pending shared editor intake. It supplies descriptive geometry and contracts, not collision, navigation, ballistics, structural or fire simulation.
