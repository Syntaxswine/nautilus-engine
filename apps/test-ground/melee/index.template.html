    Training profile

    Dexterity sets accuracy. Strength scales declared attack damage; endurance reduces damage once. Agility and endurance supply the existing AP and stamina budgets. Windup reserves costs; impact spends them, even on a miss. Interruption before impact is free and enters recovery.

    Contact uses the selected body's reach, facing cone, height and current wall clearance. This is a conservative tactical approximation. Shield guard costs stamina over time and per block; an equipped shield alone grants no immunity.
