# Native held equipment presentation

`@nautilus-engine/map-core/native-actors/melee/three` exports `prepareNativeMeleeActor`. It uses the existing native loader, the host's **same Nautilus Three renderer**, original full-resolution native paint and the reviewed original fantasy art. The first optional adapter supports the standing native goat rig and inherited native gait/checkpoint v1. It does not expand donkey/crawl support or change any existing actor API.

Supply `renderer`, `actorId` and `readPresentation: () => readMeleePresentation(state, actorId, currentHeadingDeg)`, plus the existing native loader's resource/abort/checkpoint options. Preparation starts inactive. Call `updateConfirmed` with the retained physical pose, attach `view.root` to the borrowed scene, and activate it. Use the returned presentation observation to diagnose grip error, not to confirm an impact. On every render update it resets the previous overlay, applies the retained native pose, positions the native-metre equipment from actual runtime grip sockets, then solves the original arm chains without changing bone lengths or model scale. A moving bow nock is sampled from the current articulated model. The old native rifle is hidden only by this optional view.

The root remains at the retained physical position. Heading and windup/impact/recovery are presentation queries; no pose, art socket, collision proxy or marker spends resources, rolls or damages HP. Bows/crossbows support visual draw/load independently, including empty drawing. They grant no ranged rules. Art local +Y is the long axis; +Z becomes the actor's forward direction for shields/projectiles. Main/off hands and declared two-hand use remain distinct. Artist metadata bounds are visual bounds across poses, never collision bodies.

Equipment swaps prepare both new owned models before attaching a complete candidate. Partial acquisition/attachment failures retire the candidate and keep the accepted models. Ownership is transferred before retiring old models or notifying removal observers. Reentrant/throwing cleanup drains the remaining models and the native view, retires terminally, and truthfully aggregates failures. Call `dispose` once when the retained view ends; it is idempotent. Do not dispose the borrowed renderer/scene or shared Three namespace. Save `checkpoint()` with the host's melee/character/world record; it remains the inherited native checkpoint, rather than a second gameplay save owner.

Use [melee-usage-card.json](melee-usage-card.json) and [the Night Ledger example](../../apps/test-ground/melee/main.js). Art source is the exact Architect-approved commit `0e3131c4a2becd1c9f2647bcddcaa60eb4e075d9`, whose runtime is byte-identical to approved `654f648e8936376358cf57e5733db0a2dad3b760`. Finishes/geometry remain Artist-owned. This adapter supplies held poses, not native weapon physics or a new first-person camera.
